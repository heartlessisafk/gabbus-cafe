01 How the Passport Works • Simple & Personal

Four Steps to Good Times.

01

GET YOUR PASSPORT

Ask your host for your personal Gabbu’s Passport when you dine with us at Gabbu’s.

02

VISIT GABBU’S

Come by for morning coffee & bakery treats, mountain lunches, or evening bar sessions.

03

COLLECT STAMPS

Each time you visit and share a good time, your Passport receives an official house stamp.

04

UNLOCK REWARDS

Your 5th, 7th, and 10th stamps unlock complimentary treats from our Bar, Bakery, and Kitchen.
