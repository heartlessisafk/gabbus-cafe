Coffee Pairing: What to Eat With Your Favourite Brew

Bakery secrets from our pastry chefs and head baristas: pairing buttery laminated croissants with citrus pour-overs, flat whites with almond brioche, and dark roasts with rich fudge tortes.

Words by Gabbu’s Bakery & Barista Guild
The morning harmony: flaky 72-layer laminated butter croissant and an Ethiopian light roast pour-over.

Sommeliers spend lifetimes refining how tannins in Nebbiolo interact with the marbling of aged beef. Yet for decades, coffee was served with whatever dry biscuit was lying near the cash register. Fortunately, specialty coffee has entered its gastronomic golden age. When you pair the specific acidity, body, and tasting notes of a single-origin brew with the fat, sugar, and moisture of artisan pastry, 1 + 1 equals far more than 2.

At Gabbu’s, our bakery ovens fire at 4:30 AM every day. Here is our master guide to pairing our signature bakery creations with our coffee bar.

1. The Light Roast Pour-Over + The Classic French Butter Croissant

Light-roasted African and South Indian washed Arabicas are defined by crisp, vibrant fruit acidity—think bergamot, green apple, peach, and jasmine. If you pair this delicate nectar with a heavy chocolate cake, the delicate floral notes are instantly obliterated.

Instead, pair it with our 72-layer European butter croissant. The high-fat cultured butter coats your palate, and when followed by a sip of bright, acidic pour-over, the fruit acidity cleanly cuts through the butterfat, leaving your palate refreshed, sweet, and tingling with toasted wheat complexity.

“Fat softens acidity; acidity cleanses fat. Once you understand this balance, every breakfast becomes a culinary masterclass.” — Executive Pastry Chef, Gabbu’s Bakery

2. The Silky Flat White + Spiced Apple & Cinnamon Brioche

A flat white—with its double ristretto base and micro-foamed milk—possesses natural lactose sweetness and a velvety mouthfeel. The sweet warmth of steamed milk naturally loves baking spices: nutmeg, clove, ginger, and Ceylon cinnamon.

We pair our flat whites with our seasonal Kullu Apple Tart or Cinnamon Brioche Swirl. The tart acidity of local Himachali apples balances the sweet richness of the milk foam, creating the liquid equivalent of fresh apple pie à la mode.

✦ GABBU’S PAIRING CHEAT-SHEET

• Espresso Double: Dark 70% Chocolate Walnut Torte (cuts bitterness, highlights cocoa nibs).
• Aeropress / Americano: Lemon Almond Tea Cake (citrus echoes the bean acidity).
• Cold Brew: Creamy Salted Caramel Cheesecake (rich dairy tames the concentrated brew).

3. The Bold Dark Roast / French Roast + Dark Chocolate Fudge Torte

Dark roasts contain deep, caramelized sugars, smoky oak, and roasted nut tones with virtually zero fruit acidity. If you pair a dark roast with a light fruit pastry, the pastry tastes sour. But pair it with a slice of our flourless Belgian dark chocolate torte, and magic happens.

The roasted bittersweet notes of the bean resonate with the 70% cacao solids, while the toasted Himalayan walnuts inside the cake echo the roast profile. It is deep, brooding, decadent, and intensely satisfying—especially on a rainy Manali afternoon.

Next time you stand in front of our bakery display counter, don’t hesitate to ask our baristas: “Which pastry was baked for this specific bean today?” We love making the match.
