Meet Gabbu: The Dog Behind Gabbu’s

He wasn’t just a loyal German Shepherd; he was the gentle soul of the mountains. Why this entire sanctuary carries his name, spirit, and unconditional warmth.

Words by The Founders • Dedicated to Gabbar (“Gabbu”)
Gabbar on his favourite cedar perch overlooking the Old Manali apple orchards, autumn afternoon.

If you walk through the cedar archway of our cafe, you will find his face everywhere: embossed in gold foil on our leather menus, pressed onto our passport stamps, hand-drawn on matchboxes, and cast in brass behind the bar. To casual visitors, it might look like a clever branding motif. But to us, Gabbu was never a brand. He was family.

His official name was Gabbar, but from the first day he bounded up the pine-needle trails of the Kullu valley as an oversized, floppy-eared puppy, everyone knew him simply as Gabbu. He had the unmistakable noble silhouette of a German Shepherd—deep sable mantle, warm tan paws, and erect, alert ears—but his temperament was pure quiet grace.

1. The Guardian of Slow Time

Dogs in the mountains live by a different clock than city dogs. Gabbu never rushed anything. His morning routine was sacred: walk slowly along the edge of the cedar deck, inspect the morning mist rising from the Beas river, receive a gentle head scratch from the morning baker, and then find the single patch of sun moving across the floorboards.

Whenever a guest walked in visibly burdened by travel fatigue, flight delays, or work stress, Gabbu had an uncanny intuition. He would quietly trot over, sit beside their chair with dignified composure, and press his head softly against their knee. You could visibly watch the guest’s shoulders drop, their breathing ease, and a smile return to their face.

“He taught us the most important hospitality lesson we will ever learn: that being truly welcoming has nothing to do with fancy words. It is simply about being steadfastly present.” — Founder of Gabbu’s Cafe

2. Why the Cafe Carries His Name

When we first planned to open a kitchen, bar, and bakery in Old Manali, friends suggested trendy European names, Italian coffee terms, or abstract lifestyle buzzwords. None of them felt true to the soil.

We realized that everything we wanted the space to represent—unquestioned loyalty, warmth without pretension, fierce love for good food, and a shelter from the cold mountain wind—was already personified by Gabbu. Naming the cafe after him was our promise that the spirit of loyalty and gentleness would remain baked into the walls forever.

✦ GABBU’S PROMISE TO FOUR-LEGGED FRIENDS

Because of Gabbu, our cafe has always been and will forever remain 100% pet-friendly. We keep fresh mountain spring water bowls on every deck and serve house-baked peanut butter oat treats for visiting dogs.

3. His Legacy in Every Detail

Today, even as Gabbu watches over us from the green meadows beyond, his spirit permeates every corner of the cafe:

  • The Sleeping Gabbu Footer: Every digital and physical page closes with a portrait of him curled up peacefully in dreamland.
  • The Gabbu Passport: A tribute to his wanderings through the trails of Old Manali, inviting guests to collect stamps and share in the journey.
  • The Golden Tagline: “A Dog Behind the Good Times.” A reminder that the best memories are made in humble, loyal company.

When you visit us next, take a moment to look at the photos by the entrance. And if you’re travelling with your own pup, bring them along—Gabbu would have wanted nothing more than to share the sun with them.
